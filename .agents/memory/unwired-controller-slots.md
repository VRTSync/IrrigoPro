---
name: Unwired controller slots
description: Physical numbered slots can have no wire; do not confuse that with a wired zone at zero minutes.
---

Controllers may have numbered wire slots with no wire connected. The user requested the explicit label **Not Wired** in the documenter rather than documenting these as Other.

**Why:** An unused physical slot is different from a wired zone intentionally running for zero minutes; the numbered position still exists.

**How to apply:** Preserve slot identity and numbering when recording wiring status. Changes to how unwired slots affect scheduling, completion, or wet checks need separate scope; adding the label alone does not authorize those changes.
